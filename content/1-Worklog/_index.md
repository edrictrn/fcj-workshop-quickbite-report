This page summarizes the entire worklog during the 10-week internship program of the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)**. 

The internship period was carried out from **June 01, 2026** to **August 07, 2026**, focusing on exploring core AWS cloud services and building a full-stack, Cloud-Native deployment for the **QuickBite** food ordering platform.

---

### 10-Week Internship Summary:

* **Week 1:** [Getting familiar with basic AWS & Refactoring QuickBite Backend with RBAC](1.1-week1/)
* **Week 2:** [Database Management with SQLAlchemy, Alembic Migrations & Email Utility Setup](1.2-week2/)
* **Week 3:** [Multi-containerization with Docker Compose & Local Email Simulation via Mailpit](1.3-week3/)
* **Week 4:** [Provisioning Amazon RDS PostgreSQL & Storing Menu Images on Amazon S3](1.4-week4/)
* **Week 5:** [Deploying Backend to Amazon EC2 & Hosting Static Frontend on S3 + CloudFront CDN](1.5-week5/)
* **Week 6:** [Researching Event-Driven Email Architecture with AWS Lambda & Amazon SES](1.6-week6/)
* **Week 7:** [Implementing SQL Views, Dashboard Analytics & Business CSV Export Reports](1.7-week7/)
* **Week 8:** [Building Audit Logging System, Operation Tracking & Dynamic System Configuration](1.8-week8/)
* **Week 9:** [System Monitoring with Amazon CloudWatch, Health Checks & Security Hardening](1.9-week9/)
* **Week 10:** [End-to-End Testing, Cloud Resource Cleanup, Video Demo & Final Submission](1.10-week10/)
