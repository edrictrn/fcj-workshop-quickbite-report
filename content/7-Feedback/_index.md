### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**
The working environment was fine — nothing to complain about, but nothing particularly stood out either.

**2. Support from Mentor**
My mentor (Lữ Hoàn Thiện) was very dedicated, going above and beyond whenever I needed help.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**
The work was only partly related to my major. I study Computer Engineering, which leans toward hardware, while this internship was mainly software/cloud (building and deploying QuickBite on AWS) — so it was a fairly new area compared to what I studied at school.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**
The biggest skill I picked up during this internship was how to use AWS — from the basic services to actually deploying a real application on it myself.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**
Average — I'd rate it around 5/10. Nothing negative, but nothing that left a strong impression either.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**
No internship allowance.

### Additional Questions
- **Most satisfying**: getting hands-on practice with AWS and actually building and deploying a real project, rather than just learning the theory.
- **What the company should improve**: clearer guidance from the start on what interns should be doing, instead of having to figure out the direction on your own.
- **Would recommend to a friend**: worth recommending to someone genuinely interested in software/cloud, but for someone more hardware-focused like me, the content would only be partly relevant.

### Suggestions & Expectations
- **Suggestion to improve the experience**: a clearer roadmap or guidance for interns from the very beginning of the program.
- **Would I continue this program**: not sure yet — depends on my career direction going forward.
- **Other comments**: none for now.
